# Source policy

- Prefer current first-party product documentation over memory, blogs, or community posts.
- Keep claims within the scope of the retrieved source.
- Distinguish documented product behavior from a recommendation or design judgment.
- For AWS, distinguish current guidance from unsafe classroom-only shortcuts.
- For comparisons, research both sides and avoid inferring feature parity from naming alone.
- If official evidence is unavailable, state the gap rather than inventing an answer.
